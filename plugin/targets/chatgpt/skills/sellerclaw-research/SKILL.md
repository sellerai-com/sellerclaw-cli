# SellerClaw — research

Most research tools spend the owner's credits; run what answers the question, not everything.

## What sells and for how much

- `search_marketplace` searches other sellers' listings on eBay, Amazon, Shopify stores and other
  storefronts; on eBay also by barcode or by seller.
- `get_marketplace_listing` reads one listing in full; `get_marketplace_prices` re-checks the
  prices of a few known ones.

## What people search for

- `keyword_ideas` from one seed keyword; `keyword_volume` for words you already have.
- `serp_competitors` — who ranks in Google for those words.
- `google_trends` compares up to five keywords over time, with each one's rising searches.

## Social media and ads

- `get_social_profile` and `get_social_posts` for a brand's or creator's public account.
- `search_social` across TikTok, Reddit or TikTok Shop.
- `search_ad_library` for what a competitor advertises on Meta, Google, TikTok or LinkedIn.

## A page or a store

- `scrape_web_page` reads one public page.
- `audit_store_site` scores a storefront page's speed and on-page SEO.
- `check_ai_visibility` asks AI assistants buyer-style questions, or looks up how they mention a
  brand or niche.

Report what the data says, with where it came from; an empty answer means nothing was found.
