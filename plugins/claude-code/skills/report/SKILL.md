Write the owner a short report on how the business did. Work from the analytics guide — `sellerclaw_guide(topic="analytics")` — for every store at once (`all`).

- The period comes from the owner's words; none → the last finished week (`week`: 0).
- Cover sales, orders, profit and margin, the trend against the period before, best sellers, what sold nothing and what to reorder — then the two or three things worth doing next.
- Leave out any figure the answers do not carry; never write it as zero. Say when the data is partial.

Owner's words: $ARGUMENTS
